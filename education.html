TAJO: talent workshops for young people

Through Ghent University, I take part in TAJO (Talentateliers voor jongeren), which gives young people aged 10 to 14 from vulnerable backgrounds weekly Saturday workshops on all kinds of professions. I help them discover what it's like to be a scientist.
