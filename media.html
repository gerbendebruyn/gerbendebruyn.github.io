VRT NWS: pink katydid turns green in 11 days

VRT NWS asked us to discuss a new study in Ecology on a leaf-mimicking katydid from Panama that changed from pink to fully green in just 11 days. I gave my view on what might cause the pink colour and why it could help the insect hide. (In Dutch)

Media · March 2026

Science News: platypus fur shares a surprising feature with birds

Science News covered our Biology Letters study (led by Jessica L. Dobson) showing that platypus hairs contain hollow melanosomes, pigment structures that had only been known from bird feathers and were never seen in a mammal before.

Media · September 2025

Phys.org: iridescence is more widespread in mammals than thought

Phys.org covered our Journal of the Royal Society Interface study (led by Jessica L. Dobson) showing that iridescent hair is not limited to golden moles. It shows up across many mammal groups, including rodents and shrews, produced by thin layers in the hair.

Media · March 2025

Eos Wetenschap: early mammals were small and plain brown

Eos Wetenschap covered our Science paper (led by Ruoshuang Li) on the colour of Mesozoic mammal relatives. I explain how we compared fossil melanosomes with those in modern mammal fur to reconstruct their colour, and what that says about staying hidden among dinosaurs. (In Dutch)
